Prometheus is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Open the UI:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "prometheus.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  then browse to http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}

To have Prometheus scrape a pod, annotate it with:
  prometheus.io/scrape: "true"
  prometheus.io/port: "<metrics port>"
