Check sync status:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get externalsecrets

Pods that read secrets as environment variables keep the old value until they
restart. Annotate their Deployments with reloader.stakater.com/auto: "true" and
install Stakater Reloader to restart them on every rotation.
